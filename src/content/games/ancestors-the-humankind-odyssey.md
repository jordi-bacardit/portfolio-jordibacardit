note: |-
  Ancestors: The Humankind Odyssey is one of those games that caught my attention because of how different it was from anything I had played before. Instead of following a traditional story, you are placed in control of an early human ancestor and have to survive, explore, learn, and evolve.

  I really enjoyed the feeling of discovery. You start with almost nothing and gradually learn how to use your surroundings, develop new abilities, and pass knowledge down through generations. There is something incredibly satisfying about seeing your small group slowly become more capable and watching evolution unfold through gameplay.

  And, of course, the idea of swinging through the jungle like Tarzan was definitely one of the things that caught my attention in the first place.

  It’s a game that made me appreciate how much can be done with a simple idea when the gameplay itself becomes the story.
