note: |-
  I started playing Apex Legends when it first came out, already knowing it was made by the developers behind Titanfall. That was one of the main reasons I was interested in trying it in the first place.

  I really enjoyed the game, especially its fast-paced gameplay and movement, which made it feel familiar to anyone who had played Titanfall before. It was a battle royale I had a lot of fun with, but I never ended up spending too much time on it.

  For me, Apex Legends was mostly about playing with friends, jumping into matches together, having a laugh, and seeing how far we could get. I may not have put hundreds of hours into it, but it still holds a place in my gaming memories.
