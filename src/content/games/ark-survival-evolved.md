note: |-
  ARK: Survival Evolved was one of the first PC games I played on my own computer, which already made it a pretty special game for me. What initially caught my attention was the idea of being stranded in a world full of dinosaurs, having to survive, explore, and eventually tame and ride them.

  I really enjoyed that feeling at the beginning. Starting with nothing, discovering new creatures, building a small base, and slowly learning how to survive in a world where dinosaurs were everywhere felt like the perfect survival experience.

  Eventually, though, I stopped playing because I got burned out. As I played more, the game started to lose some of that original feeling for me. What initially felt like a primal survival game focused on dinosaurs gradually became something much bigger and more complicated, and I personally felt that it moved further away from the experience that had attracted me in the first place.

  Still, ARK was an important game for me, especially because it was one of my first PC gaming experiences on my own computer.
