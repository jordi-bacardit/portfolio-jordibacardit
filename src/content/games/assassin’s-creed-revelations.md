  What made me want to play Revelations the most was the idea of seeing Ezio and Altaïr connected in the same story. After playing the previous games and getting to know both characters, seeing their stories come together was something I really wanted to experience.

  I also enjoyed seeing an older Ezio and how his story was coming to an end. Exploring Constantinople and experiencing another historical period was, as always, one of my favourite parts.

  But honestly, the main reason I played it was Altaïr and Ezio. Seeing these two characters, from completely different generations of the Assassin Brotherhood, connected through the same story was something really special to me.
