note: |-
  Origins felt like a new type of Assassin’s Creed for me. It was very different from the previous games, especially with the RPG elements and the way the game world worked.

  I have to say that knowing this was set before the Assassin Brotherhood was actually created made me feel like some of the original Assassin’s Creed essence was missing. There was something different about playing a game called Assassin’s Creed when the Assassins didn’t really exist yet.

  But Bayek was a character I really enjoyed. His story, his personality and the reasons behind everything he did made me want to follow his journey and see where it would take him.

  And then there was Ancient Egypt. Being able to roam around Egypt, explore the pyramids, discover ancient cities and see how people lived during that period was amazing to me. The exploration was probably my favourite part of the game.

  Having Rome involved as well made the historical side even more interesting. Once again, what kept me playing was the opportunity to explore and experience another period of history through a game.

  Origins changed the formula, but Bayek and the opportunity to explore Ancient Egypt made it a really memorable Assassin’s Creed experience for me.
