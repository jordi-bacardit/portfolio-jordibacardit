note: |-
  Valhalla was another Assassin’s Creed game that really caught my attention because of its Viking setting. I loved seeing the Vikings arrive in England, the raids, the settlements, and the way the game showed their attempt to build a new life there.

  I really enjoyed the new stealth mechanics too, especially being able to use the cloak and blend into the environment. The camp management and resource system also made the experience feel different from the previous games. Going on raids to gather resources and then using them to develop the settlement gave me a reason to keep exploring.

  Following Eivor’s journey was another part I really enjoyed. But one of my favourite things was the way the game represented Viking mythology. Seeing how their beliefs and mythology were brought into the world made me want to learn more about it.

  Actually, Valhalla made me go back and watch the Vikings TV series because I wanted more of that world and history after playing the game. That probably says a lot about how much I enjoyed the Viking setting.
