note: |-
  ATLAS was a game I tried mainly because of its pirate theme and because I could play it with friends. Coming from the developers of ARK: Survival Evolved, I was interested in seeing what they could do with the survival formula in a world focused on exploration, ships and pirates.

  The idea itself was really appealing to me. Building ships, sailing across the sea, exploring different islands and trying to survive with friends sounded like exactly the kind of experience I would enjoy.

  Unfortunately, it never really clicked for me. I played it with friends and had some fun moments, but in the end it didn’t manage to keep me playing for long. The pirate setting was probably the thing that attracted me the most, but the game itself just didn’t end up working for me.
