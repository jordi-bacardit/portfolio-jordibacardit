note: |-
  I really enjoyed Brotherhood because it continued Ezio’s story and let me see him become a true Master Assassin. After everything that happened in Assassin’s Creed II, it was great to see how much he had grown and how he started taking a bigger role in the Brotherhood.

  But what I love most about Assassin’s Creed is the history behind the games. Being able to walk around Renaissance Rome, explore the city, climb its buildings and see historical places brought to life is something I really enjoy.

  I also have to mention the multiplayer. I had a lot of fun with it, especially because it was so different from the usual multiplayer games I was playing at the time.

  History has always been something I love, and being able to experience different periods of history through a game is what makes Assassin’s Creed so special to me.
