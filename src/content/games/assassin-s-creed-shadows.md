note: |-
  I’ve always been fascinated by Japanese culture and history, so I was really looking forward to Shadows. Being able to explore Japan during such an important period of its history was something I had wanted to see in an Assassin’s Creed game for a long time.

  I especially enjoyed the historical events surrounding Oda Nobunaga’s death, the conflict that followed, and the rise of Toyotomi Hideyoshi. Seeing that period represented through the game and exploring how Japan was changing was one of my favourite parts.

  And, of course, simply exploring Japan was incredible. Walking through Osaka, Kyoto and the other settlements, travelling through forests and seeing the castles and landscapes made the world really enjoyable to explore.

  I was hoping to see a little more of Tokugawa Ieyasu, but I really liked the inclusion of Hattori Hanzo. Knowing that he was historically connected to Tokugawa Ieyasu and seeing him included as part of the Assassin’s world was a really nice touch for me.

  Just like with the other Assassin’s Creed games, what I enjoy most is being able to explore a historical period that I’m genuinely interested in and experience it through a game.
