note: |-
  For me, Black Flag is one of the best games in the series. I loved almost everything about it. Being a pirate, having your own ship, sailing across the sea, discovering islands and looking for new adventures was exactly the kind of experience I could get lost in.

  I really liked Edward Kenway’s story too, especially seeing how he slowly became involved with the Assassins. Knowing that he was also Connor’s grandfather made the connection with Assassin’s Creed III even more interesting for me.

  I remember playing Black Flag almost every summer. Sometimes I didn’t even care about the main story. I just wanted to get on my ship, sail through the sea and imagine myself living that life, travelling from place to place in search of adventures.

  Years later, I played it again with the resynced version, and that feeling of freedom came back even stronger. Being able to sail between settlements with almost no loading screens makes the world feel much more connected. It makes the experience of simply being on your ship, travelling across the sea and deciding where to go next even more enjoyable.

  That feeling of freedom is probably what I remember the most about Black Flag. It made me want to be out there sailing, exploring and seeing what was waiting beyond the horizon.
