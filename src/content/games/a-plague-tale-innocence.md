note: |-
  At first, A Plague Tale: Innocence didn’t really convince me. I wasn’t sure if it was going to be a game I would enjoy, but after giving it a proper chance, everything changed.

  The atmosphere and setting slowly drew me in, but it was the story that really made the game memorable for me. As I became more invested in Amicia and Hugo’s journey, I found myself genuinely wanting to know what would happen next.

  It was one of those games that reminded me not to judge a game too quickly. Giving it a second chance is what eventually made me appreciate the series and led me to play A Plague Tale: Requiem.
