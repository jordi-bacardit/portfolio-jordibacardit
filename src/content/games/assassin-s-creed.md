note: |-
  Assassin’s Creed was the first game in the series I ever played, and it immediately captivated me. It combined two things I have always loved: history and video games.

  The historical setting, the open world, and the freedom to explore made the game feel like a world I could actually get lost in. But what really made it stand out to me was the parkour. Being able to freely move across rooftops, climb buildings, and explore the cities from above was something I had never experienced in quite the same way before.

  It was the combination of history, exploration, open-world gameplay, and parkour that made Assassin’s Creed so memorable for me and got me interested in the rest of the series.
