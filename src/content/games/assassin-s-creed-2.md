note: |-
  Assassin’s Creed II was the game that really made me fall in love with the series. I loved Ezio’s story and watching him grow and evolve throughout the game, from a young man seeking answers to becoming a true Assassin.

  I also really enjoyed the continuation of Desmond Miles’ story. Seeing how his experiences in the Animus connected with Ezio’s journey made the overall story feel much bigger than just what was happening in Renaissance Italy.

  One of my favourite parts was simply roaming around Florence and exploring the city as it might have looked during the Renaissance. Climbing its buildings, running across the rooftops, and wandering through its streets made the historical setting feel much more alive.

  The combination of Ezio’s personal journey, Desmond’s story, the historical setting, and the freedom to explore Renaissance Italy made Assassin’s Creed II one of the games that stayed with me the most from the series.
