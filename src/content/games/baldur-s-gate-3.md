  Everything about it hooked me: the story, the decision-making, the characters and the setting. A single decision could change the course of the whole story, and that gave me an incredible sense of freedom. I’d put it among my top games, and I always recommend it.

  I was reluctant to play turn-based games until I tried this one, and I fell in love with it.

  I’ve always believed that story and setting are key to getting people hooked on an RPG. They’re essential, and I think this game nailed it, not only because of that but because of everything Baldur’s Gate 3 is. On top of that, it comes from D&D, and that alone is incredible.
