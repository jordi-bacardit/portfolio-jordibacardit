note: |-
  Assassin’s Creed III was another game I really enjoyed because of its historical setting. Playing as Connor during the American Revolutionary War let me experience another major historical event through a game, which is one of the things I love most about Assassin’s Creed.

  I really liked exploring the world, seeing the colonies during that period, and experiencing the events of the Revolution through Connor’s story.

  But seeing Desmond die was honestly hard for me. I had followed his story since the first Assassin’s Creed, and after everything that happened with Ezio and the previous games, his death felt like the end of something important. It felt like the saga had lost a character that had been at the centre of everything for so long.

  Even though I enjoyed Connor and the historical setting, Desmond’s story was a big part of what made the earlier games special to me, so seeing it end definitely changed how I felt about the series.
