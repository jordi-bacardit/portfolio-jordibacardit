note: |-
  I started playing Arma 3 because of its milsim experience. I was fascinated by how much the game focused on realism, coordination, communication, and teamwork rather than simply running and shooting.

  My first serious experience with the game was joining a Star Wars milsim clan based around the 41st Clone Troopers. I eventually became an anti-tank trooper, which gave me a specific role within the squad and made the whole experience feel much more like being part of an actual unit.

  Later, I joined another milsim community based around the Spanish Legion. Both experiences showed me how much I enjoyed the organizational and teamwork side of military simulation.

  Arma 3 is probably one of the games where I appreciated the community and the way people created their own experiences just as much as the game itself. The milsim aspect has always been what kept me coming back.
