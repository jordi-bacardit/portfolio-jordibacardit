  The first video game I ever played. I was around six years old when I first started playing Call of Duty 2, and I still remember those first moments.

  Back then, I barely knew how to use a keyboard and mouse. I could move the mouse, and the only key I really knew how to use was W to move forward. I had no idea what I was doing, but I was completely fascinated by the game.

  Looking back, it’s funny to think that this was where my journey with video games began. From barely knowing how to move my character to eventually spending thousands of hours playing and creating games myself, Call of Duty 2 will always have a special place in my gaming history.
