  ARC Raiders was another game I mostly played with friends. We had a lot of fun jumping in together, exploring, fighting the ARC machines, and trying to make it out with our loot.

  I didn’t end up spending too much time with it, though. I have to admit that games like this always bring out my competitive side a little too much. Somehow, I always end up going from “let’s have some fun” to full rage mode after a few bad runs. Hahaha.

  Still, the moments I spent playing it with friends were definitely worth it. Sometimes the best part of these games isn’t the game itself, but the chaos and laughs you get along the way.
