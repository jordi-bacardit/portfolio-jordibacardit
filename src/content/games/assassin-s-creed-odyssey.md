note: |-
  Odyssey was another Assassin’s Creed game that I really enjoyed because of its historical setting. Just like Origins, being able to explore an ancient civilization was one of the main reasons I loved it.

  It came out at a really interesting time for me because I was studying History of Art and Philosophy in high school. So while I was learning about Ancient Greece at school, I was also able to explore it in a completely different way through a game. I chose Alexios as my character, and following his story while exploring Ancient Greece made the experience even more memorable.

  Seeing places like Athens and Sparta, and coming across historical figures such as Pericles and other philosophers and important people, made it even more interesting. I loved seeing how Athens and Sparta worked in completely different ways, from Athenian democracy to Sparta’s military society. It was fascinating to see things I was studying in class represented inside a game that I could actually explore.

  I also played the DLCs, and they took the game in a completely different direction. The Elysium Fields were probably my favourite part. It felt like walking into something from a fantasy book, with this beautiful and almost surreal version of the Greek afterlife. Then going through Hades and eventually reaching Atlantis made the experience even more interesting.

  For me, Odyssey was a great mix of history, exploration and mythology. Playing it while I was studying these subjects made the experience even more special, because I could see things I was learning about and then actually explore them in a game.
