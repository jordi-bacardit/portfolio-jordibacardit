note: |-
  The first RPG I ever played. I think I was around 11 or 12 when I first started playing Skyrim, and at the time I had no idea how much this game would influence my taste in games.

  The freedom to explore the world, create my own character, choose how I wanted to play, and simply get lost in Skyrim was completely new to me. I could spend hours exploring, completing quests, discovering new places, or just wandering around without really knowing where I was going.

  Looking back, Skyrim was probably the game that introduced me to what RPGs could really be. It opened the door to a genre that would eventually become one of my favourites.
